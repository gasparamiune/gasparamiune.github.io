01The project

A project to learn computer vision by building something fun. You steer a spaceship with your hand in front of a webcam, close your fist to catch floating astronauts for points, and avoid black holes that take points away.

MediaPipe finds the hand's landmarks in every frame; an open hand moves the cursor and a closed fist acts as a click.

02What I did

  • Built the camera pipeline, the gesture logic and the game loop.
  • Structured the code as menu, hand tracker, game classes, settings and assets.
