01The project

An interdisciplinary SDU project running until December 2026. Instead of a real refrigeration circuit, the bench uses adjustable sensor-simulation circuits and switches to represent temperature, pressure, defrost demand and a sensor fault.

A controller application calculates superheat, commands a valve, runs a timed defrost and manages alarms, while a stepper-driven pointer shows the valve position. A validation tool guides an operator through test scenarios and saves traceable results.

02What I did

  • Working in a team of eleven from many disciplines and countries, with a company partner.
  • Deliverables: an integrated demonstrator, calibrated interfaces, controller and validation software, and test evidence.
